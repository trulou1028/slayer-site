The contact sheet

Four films, one program, one laptop. Every frame carries its render facts, so the claim proves itself.

A camera with a darkroom attached.

A scene is a set. A shot is a job. Nothing about either needs you to write Go.

  1. 01
    Write the shot

    A set is a world in YAML, measured in metres. A job is the camera, time, soundtrack and outputs.

  2. 02
    Trace the light

    Stills run on the CPU. Metal takes over on a Mac. Long renders resume after an interruption.

  3. 03
    Develop the film

    PNG, MP4 with a synthesized score, or raw linear light you re-expose later without re-tracing.
